# Public GitHub Pages deployment

The owner explicitly authorized publishing all 1,811 existing case records with the fields below on October 12, 2026, after reviewing the exact fields, counts and public availability. This supersedes the earlier approval limited to pseudonymous medical records.

Approved fields: hashed Case ID, disease, reporting year, morbidity week, full patient name, age, birth date, sex, DRU / reporting facility, house / street / sitio / purok address, barangay, city / municipality, province, first consultation date, onset date, admitted Yes / No, classification, outcome / death, laboratory result and test type, dengue clinical category, leptospirosis exposure category and place of exposure. These identifiable patient medical records are publicly downloadable through the repository and website. Raw source identifiers, patient numbers, Epi IDs, other workbook columns and workbook files are excluded.

Names, birth dates, DRUs and admission status are available for all 1,811 records. The source files have 61 blank street addresses and 488 blank first-consultation dates; these remain blank. The six new profile fields fill only missing values for matching existing Case IDs. Case totals, nonblank local edits, clinical data and locally deleted cases are preserved. Overlapping records from the two lepto files match by Case ID rather than creating new rows. Different Case IDs remain separate.

The 2026 dengue threshold baseline contains adjusted weekly aggregate counts for 2021–2025. The profile update does not change threshold calculations or previously published medical values.

The seed merges once per browser and preserves saved edits and manually entered cases. Later deletions are not restored on reload. New entries and changes remain in that browser; they are not synchronized across visitors. Backups can be exported and imported from Report & print.

Deploy through GitHub Actions. The build verifies the approved dataset count, fields and years, and copies only the listed app assets. Do not commit raw workbooks, backups, or generated reports.

Leptospirosis reports contain an A4 landscape bulletin followed by three Long/Folio portrait detail pages (8.5 × 13 inches). Dengue reports contain an A4 landscape bulletin followed by two Legal portrait detail pages (8.5 × 14 inches). The dashboard and reports calculate the summaries, age profiles, classifications and barangay totals from the selected disease, reporting year and morbidity weeks. Bulletin barangay tables show cases and deaths; detailed comparisons show cases and deaths for both years.

Dengue actions taken and recommendations default to the supplied DENGUE ACTIONS TAKEN document and remain editable per reporting year/week. Leptospirosis actions retain the supplied MESU text and prevention recommendations. Both reports include the supplied preparers and municipal health officer on the last page. Notes and signatories are included in data backups. Older population settings are retained in backups but are no longer displayed as table columns.

Manual entry, Batch upload and backup import check for duplicate Case IDs, including letter case, spacing and source IDs corresponding to published pseudonymous IDs. Manual duplicates are rejected, batch duplicates are skipped, and a backup containing duplicate IDs is rejected before replacement. Different IDs are not automatically treated as the same person based only on shared demographic fields. Existing saved records are not automatically removed.

Present report opens the currently selected disease and reporting period as a full-screen slide viewer. Use Previous/Next or arrow keys, Home/End, and Escape to exit. Fit page shows an entire page; Fit width allows scrolling through portrait pages. A full-window viewer works when browser fullscreen is unavailable. Print layouts and page sizes are unchanged.

The case registry and Batch upload support patient name, date of birth, DRU, street address, first consultation date and admission status. Lab Count displays the recorded laboratory result (Positive / Negative, or an existing pending/unknown status). The approved seed includes these profile details. New entries, uploads and edits stay in the user's browser and downloaded backups; they are not automatically published or synchronized. Batch upload recognizes separate first/middle/family-name columns and the supplied workbook's reporting-facility, street and clinical-date headers.

The owner approved Measles publication on October 12, 2026, and subsequently clarified that records in different years with different Case IDs must both be retained. The corrected Measles seed contains 19 records: all 6 rows from the 2025 worksheet and 13 rows from the 2026 worksheet. This brings the public seed to 1,830 records across three diseases. Approved Measles fields are hashed Case ID, disease, reporting year, morbidity week, full name, birthday, age, sex, DRU, street address, barangay, municipality, province, first consultation date, rash onset date, admission status, reporting classification, outcome, laboratory result/test, original source classification and laboratory interpretation. All first-consultation dates and the six 2025 DRUs are absent from the source and remain blank. Raw source identifiers, caregiver/contact information and unrelated workbook fields are not published.

Five rows in the worksheet named 2025 contain MorbidityYear 2026 but have different source IDs from the matching names/weeks in the 2026 worksheet. The owner's latest clarification supersedes the earlier merge decision: retain these five as separate 2025 cases using the worksheet year. The original workbook and clinical dates are unchanged. Names and weeks do not establish a duplicate. Hashed aliases now link only each of those five source IDs to its own corrected 2025 ID, never to a different ID in 2026. Reimporting either source sheet skips its existing IDs. Existing browsers receive only the five additions for this release, preserving earlier edits and deletions. The client reporting rule is Negative = Suspect and Positive / Equivocal = Confirmed. The owner explicitly confirmed including the Rubella-positive row as Confirmed under this rule; the original interpretation remains visible. Blank lab results with no entered classification remain unclassified. These configured reporting categories are identified as client mapping in the UI and printed report.

Measles uses one A4 landscape bulletin and one presentation slide, with weekly cases, same-period comparison, Confirmed/Suspect counts, age/sex proportions and barangay cases/deaths. It has no portrait detail pages. Its historical baseline is separate from the other diseases, and missing historical data is not treated as zero. The workbook does not provide enough historical years to draw automatic Measles thresholds. Existing dengue and leptospirosis reports retain their page arrangements.
